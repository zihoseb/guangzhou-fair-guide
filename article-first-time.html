First Time at Canton Fair? Complete 2026 Guide

📅 May 14, 2026 ⏱️ 12 min read 👁️ 2,847 views

Welcome to the Canton Fair! As the largest trade fair in China and one of the most important in the world, it can be overwhelming for first-timers. This comprehensive guide will walk you through everything you need to know to have a successful and stress-free experience.

What is the Canton Fair?

The Canton Fair, officially known as the China Import and Export Fair, was founded in 1957. Held biannually in Guangzhou, it's the oldest, largest, and most comprehensive trade fair in China. Each session attracts over 200,000 buyers from more than 210 countries and regions.

💡 Pro Tip

Plan your trip 2-3 months in advance. Hotel prices surge 2-3x during the fair, and flights fill up quickly!

Phase 1: Pre-Registration

Before you arrive, there are several critical steps you need to complete:

  • Online Registration: Visit the official Canton Fair website and complete your pre-registration at least 1 month before the fair
  • Documentation: Prepare your passport, invitation letter, and business registration documents
  • Badge Application: Apply for your buyer badge online to save time at the venue
  • Visa Arrangements: Apply for your Chinese visa well in advance

Registration Process Step-by-Step

  1. Go to cantonfair.org.cn and create an account
  2. Upload your passport photo and personal information
  3. Wait for verification (usually 2-3 business days)
  4. Receive confirmation email with QR code
  5. Bring QR code and passport to collect badge on-site

Getting to the Venue

The Canton Fair Complex is located at Pazhou Island, Guangzhou. Here are your main transportation options:

From Baiyun International Airport

  • Metro: Take Line 3 to Kecun, transfer to Line 8 to Pazhou Station (about 1 hour, ¥9)
  • Airport Express Bus: Direct buses to the complex (about 1.5 hours, ¥28)
  • Taxi: About 45 minutes, ¥150-200

From Guangzhou South Railway Station

  • Metro: Line 2 to Changgang, transfer to Line 8 (about 50 minutes, ¥7)
  • Taxi: About 30 minutes, ¥80-120
🚇 Metro Tip

Pazhou Station (Exit A) drops you right at the main entrance. Get a rechargeable metro card (Yangcheng Tong) for convenience!

Navigating the Complex

The complex is massive - nearly 1.2 million square meters of exhibition space! It's divided into three main areas:

  • Area A: Electronics & Home Appliances, Lighting, Vehicles & Accessories
  • Area B: Machinery, Hardware & Tools, Building Materials
  • Area C: Daily Consumer Goods, Gifts, Home Decorations

What to Bring

  • Passport and registration QR code
  • Comfortable walking shoes (you'll walk 10-15km/day!)
  • Portable power bank (charging stations are scarce)
  • Business cards (both English and Chinese versions)
  • Notebook or tablet for notes
  • Water bottle (water is available at fountains)
  • Small snacks (food is expensive and crowded)

On-Site Tips

  1. Arrive early (9:00 AM) to avoid crowds and heat
  2. Download the official app for maps and exhibitor info
  3. Take photos of booth numbers and supplier contacts
  4. Schedule meetings in advance - don't try to "wing it"
  5. Stay hydrated and take regular breaks
  6. Don't just collect business cards - have meaningful conversations
